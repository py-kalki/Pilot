Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview and Commitment

At Pilot ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the confidentiality of your career and technical interview preparation data. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and safeguard information when you use our website and web application platforms (app.usepilot.cfd).

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with Pilot, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials when creating an account.
  • Preparation Inputs: Job descriptions, target company URLs, resumes, candidate role levels, and personal interview notes.
  • Usage Analytics: Assessment response intervals, flashcard spaced-repetition calibrations, error logs, and session telemetry to improve pipeline speed.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly to:

  • Synthesize personalized, requirement-traceable interview question banks.
  • Execute deterministic coverage verification checks against provided job requirements.
  • Preserve your edit-safe interview preparation history and spaced repetition schedules.
  • Secure and authenticate platform sessions against unauthorized access.

4. Zero Third-Party Selling Guarantee

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, resumes, or interview prep materials to third-party data brokers, recruiters, or advertisers under any circumstances.

5. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive communications and preparation kits are transmitted using industry-standard TLS encryption. Data stored within Pilot databases is safeguarded using robust access controls, encrypted volumes, and regular security audits.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You maintain full sovereignty over your preparation data. You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your account and all associated interview kits at any time by contacting our support team.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data requests regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at: privacy@usepilot.cfd.
